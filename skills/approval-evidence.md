# Skill: Approval Evidence Management

**Objective:** Record human and external approvals in an auditable, scope-specific form.

**Required inputs:** Approval request, artifact index, approver response, evidence references.

**Execution steps:**

1. Identify decision, scope, artifact versions and conditions.
2. Record approver identity/role, date, decision and durable evidence reference.
3. For DPO/EO, create/update an external-approval entry.
4. Record Box status as uploaded only with URL/receipt/export/user confirmation evidence.

**Output format:** Workflow-state approval entry, decision-log entry and external-approval entry when applicable.

**Quality checklist:** Explicit decision is captured; evidence is linked; conditions/rejections are retained; unknowns remain unknown.

**Restrictions:** Do not infer consent, substitute an AI conclusion for approval, or claim a Box upload without evidence.
