# Information Architecture — Entry and Exit States

| Journey | Entry states | Successful exit | Safe alternate / incomplete exit | Unknowns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | Direct/nav entry, saved application resume. | Approved purchase handoff after verified option selection. | Save/exit, manual-underwriting route, support, integration recovery. | Purchase flow, decision timing and exact eligibility rules. |
| Policy hub | Authenticated policy entry. | Return to hub after document view/approved servicing. | Authentication recovery or support. | Identity method and approved servicing actions. |
| Claim | Authenticated claim start or approved alternate verification. | Submission confirmation, then tracked claim with documented decision when issued. | Emergency-services exit, save incomplete claim, support, integration recovery. | Alternate verification route, completeness rule, owners/stages/update windows. |
| Support | Contextual/global help entry. | Channel handoff, callback/case receipt or resolved return to task. | Escalation route, unavailable-support notice. | Channels, hours, SLA, accommodations and escalation policy. |

**Cross-cutting exit rule:** No state may imply a premium, approval, coverage, payment amount/outcome or timeline guarantee. (BRD §1; BRD-012, BRD-019.)
