# Skill: Presentation Generation

**Objective:** Assemble a decision-ready review presentation from approved UX evidence without presenting it as an approval, release, or external upload.

**Required inputs:** Approved phase artifacts, traceability matrix, gate results, approval records, residual-risk register and audience/context.

**Execution steps:**

1. Identify the decision to be reviewed and the artifact versions in scope.
2. Summarize requirement coverage, evidence, open risks, gate state and external-approval status.
3. Cite source artifact paths and BRD IDs where applicable.
4. Label proposed, pending, rejected and not-evidenced items clearly.
5. Save the review pack in `presentation/` and link it from the final approval pack.

**Output format:** A review presentation or presentation-ready Markdown pack with scope, evidence index, traceability summary, decisions requested, residual risks and approval-status statement.

**Quality checklist:** Sources and versions are identifiable; BRD links are present where applicable; approval status is accurate; DPO/EO and Box status are evidence-based; no unsupported release claim appears.

**Restrictions:** Do not invent findings or approvals, claim that a presentation is approved, or claim a Box upload without a verified evidence reference.
