# Business Problem Statement

**Source:** BRD §2, P-01–P-06; BRD §3  
**Traceability:** No single BRD requirement applies; this is a source-grounded synthesis of the stated problem, not a new requirement.

Customers may find property insurance unaffordable, difficult to apply for, difficult to claim, and inadequately supported. They may also distrust unclear intermediary and commission structures. The BRD identifies resulting abandonment, underinsurance, lapse/price shopping, errors, incomplete or delayed claims, repeat support contacts, reduced confidence, churn, and added manual operating cost. The sixth problem is missing, so the problem scope remains incomplete.

The proposed product direction is to reduce avoidable friction and make eligible choices and claim progress clearer without promising insurance, underwriting, payment, amount, or timing outcomes that are subject to policy terms, evidence, decisions and law.
