# Information Architecture — Content and State Inventory

| State / content category | Required content or behavior | Destination nodes | Traceability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage and choice content | Approved plain-language explanation, wording, exclusions, premium/payment/deductible/limits/fees/disclosures and choice consequence. | IA-02, IA-05, IA-06 | BRD-001, BRD-008–BRD-012, BRD-031 |
| Application progress | Save/resume/review, required-data explanation, permitted prefill provenance/correction and accessible validation. | IA-03, IA-04, IA-20 | BRD-002–BRD-006, BRD-026 |
| Authenticated policy context | Policy summary, documents, dates, deductible, approved servicing and contact routes. | IA-07–IA-09 | BRD-011, BRD-027 |
| Claim safety/reporting | Emergency distinction, incident/safety information, declarations, evidence labels and resumability. | IA-10–IA-12 | BRD-013–BRD-016 |
| Claim progress | Reference, stage, next action, owner, update window when known, requests, communications and notifications. | IA-13–IA-15 | BRD-017, BRD-018, BRD-020 |
| Decision and escalation | Approved decision explanation, payment status when available, appeal/escalation route. | IA-16 | BRD-019 |
| Support and history | Channels, hours, accommodations, context-aware summary, callback/case, history and feedback. | IA-17–IA-19 | BRD-021–BRD-024 |
| Recovery / integration | Delayed or unavailable integration, duplicate prevention, safe retry/recovery and supported interruption paths. | IA-20; invoked from all affected nodes | BRD-005, BRD-026, BRD-029 |
| Privacy, rules and automation governance | Approved authentication/authorization, data protection, configurable jurisdiction rules, governed automation and version/source trace. | Cross-cutting; IA-07, IA-20 and all content nodes | BRD-027–BRD-031 |
