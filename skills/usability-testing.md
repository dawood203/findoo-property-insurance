# Skill: Usability Testing

**Objective:** Plan and document authorized testing of priority customer tasks.

**Required inputs:** Approved test-ready artifacts, traceability matrix, research authorization/consent requirements, participant criteria.

**Execution steps:**

1. Define research questions, task scripts, success criteria and data handling.
2. Confirm authorization and consent before collecting participant data.
3. Record observations verbatim or as evidence-linked notes.
4. Synthesize findings, severity, limitations and requirement impact.

**Output format:** Test plan, anonymized session/observation records, findings register and limitations statement.

**Quality checklist:** Tasks trace to requirements; participant facts are evidenced; findings distinguish observation from inference; sensitive data is minimized.

**Restrictions:** Do not fabricate sessions, participants, metrics, consent, or statistically unsupported conclusions.
