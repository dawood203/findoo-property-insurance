# Usability Testing — Evidence Boundaries and Assumptions

## Evidence categories

| Category | Available evidence | What may be concluded |
|---|---|---|
| AI heuristic findings | Review of BRD, approved IA and detailed UI specifications. | Specification risks and missing dependencies only. |
| Developer review findings | None. | No technical/implementation conclusion. |
| Actual usability test findings | None. | No claim about user behavior, task success, satisfaction, timing or comprehension. |
| Existing-versus-proposed comparison | None. | No consistency or regression conclusion. |

## Unverified assumptions

- A future testable prototype or implemented screen set can represent the proposed IA/UI states.
- Appropriate authorization, consent, recruitment and data handling will be established before participant research.
- Confirmed target market, jurisdiction, language, devices and accessibility standard will inform participant criteria and test cases.
- Approved policy, claim, decision, payment, emergency and support content will be available for realistic scenarios.

These are assumptions for test planning only; they are not approvals or actual research evidence.
