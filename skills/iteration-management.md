# Skill: Iteration Management

**Objective:** Convert approved evidence into controlled, traceable design improvements.

**Required inputs:** Approved findings, prior artifacts, decision log and traceability matrix.

**Execution steps:**

1. Group findings by task, severity, requirement and risk.
2. Propose changes and record expected effect and trade-offs.
3. Obtain required change approval before altering approved scope.
4. Update artifacts, trace links, dispositions and retest needs.

**Output format:** Change log, finding-disposition table, revised artifact index and retest plan.

**Quality checklist:** Each change has evidence; rejected/deferred findings have rationale; no unapproved scope creep.

**Restrictions:** Do not represent a change as validated until evidence exists; do not silently overwrite decisions.
