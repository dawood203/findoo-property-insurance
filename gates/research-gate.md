# Gate: Research

**Entry criteria:** BRD/source files available; Research phase explicitly authorized.  
**Exit criteria:** BRD analysis completed; all explicit FR/NFRs have trace IDs; facts/assumptions/gaps distinguished; research outputs indexed.  
**Evidence required:** Analysis, traceability matrix, open-question list.  
**Explicit approval required:** User approval to begin Define.  
**Failure rule:** Missing source coverage, invented facts, or unresolved traceability blocks Define.
