# Gate: Iteration

**Entry criteria:** Testing gate passed and user authorized Iteration.  
**Exit criteria:** Finding dispositions and revisions are traceable; residual issues and retest needs are declared; no unapproved scope change.  
**Evidence required:** Change log, revised artifacts, disposition table, retest plan.  
**Explicit approval required:** User approval to begin UX Validation & Approval.  
**Failure rule:** Unresolved critical findings or untraceable changes block validation.
