# Skill: Problem and Scope Definition

**Objective:** Produce an approved-evidence-based definition of problem, scope and outcomes.

**Required inputs:** Approved research outputs, BRD, stakeholder decisions.

**Execution steps:**

1. State the documented problem and intended outcomes.
2. Separate in-scope, out-of-scope, proposed and undecided items.
3. Propose measures only when targets/baselines are not approved.
4. Log dependencies, risks, assumptions and questions.

**Output format:** Definition brief with trace IDs, scope table, measure proposal and decision log entries.

**Quality checklist:** Every claim has evidence or proposal label; no invented KPI target; unresolved issues preserved.

**Restrictions:** Do not approve scope, change BRD requirements, or infer regulatory/business commitments.
