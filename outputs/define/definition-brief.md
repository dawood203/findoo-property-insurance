# Define Phase — Definition Brief

**Source basis:** Approved Phase 1 Research outputs and `input/brd/property-insurance-brd.md` v0.1  
**Status:** Definition proposal for review; not an approved product scope or compliance decision.

## Documented problem

Customers report that property insurance is unaffordable, hard to apply for, hard to claim and poorly supported; they may distrust opaque intermediary/commission structures. The BRD connects these problems to abandonment, inadequate cover, incomplete claims, repeat contacts, lower confidence, churn and manual operating cost. The sixth problem remains unspecified. (BRD §2; P-01–P-06.)

## Product direction and intended outcomes

The BRD’s primary goal is an easy, guided, transparent journey for a policyholder to access and progress a property-insurance claim. Supporting outcomes are understandable cover, reduced avoidable application effort, comparable verified options, visible claim progress and accessible human support. (BRD §3.)

## Definition proposal

For the purposes of downstream review, organize the customer-facing direction around three documented tasks: obtain understandable cover, start/progress a claim, and resolve a support issue. This is a **proposal derived from the BRD journeys**, not a new requirement. The MVP recommendation is customer-facing residential claims plus simplified quote/application; it remains subject to approval. (BRD §§6, 8.)

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not promise premium, underwriting, coverage, payment amount, payment outcome or timing. (BRD §1; BRD-012, BRD-019.)
- Use only verified eligible options, approved content/disclosures and permitted data sources. (BRD-001, BRD-004, BRD-007–BRD-012.)
- Preserve a human-support route and distinguish emergency services from insurance support. (BRD-014, BRD-021–BRD-023.)
- Do not treat this definition as legal, insurance, privacy, pricing, claims or external approval. (BRD §13.)

## Unresolved definition decisions

Operating model, geography, property segment, insurer/policy types, launch channels/languages, support commitments, accessibility standard and systems of record are not confirmed. See `scope-record.md` and `success-measure-proposal.md`.
