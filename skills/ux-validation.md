# Skill: UX Validation

**Objective:** Determine whether the evidence pack supports a human readiness decision.

**Required inputs:** Final artifacts, all gate results, traceability matrix, testing/iteration evidence, risk and approval logs.

**Execution steps:**

1. Check requirement coverage and validation evidence.
2. Identify residual risks, gaps and conditional items.
3. Verify gate, DPO/EO and upload-evidence records.
4. Prepare a decision-ready summary without making the decision.

**Output format:** Validation report, coverage summary, residual-risk register and approval-pack index.

**Quality checklist:** No blank evidence fields disguised as passed; external approvals accurately stated; trace links resolve.

**Restrictions:** Do not grant approval, declare production readiness, or claim external/upload actions without evidence.
