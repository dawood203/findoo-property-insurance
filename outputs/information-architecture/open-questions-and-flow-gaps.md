## Flow completeness note

The approved architecture covers the three BRD journeys and their visible recovery routes. It does **not** define a complete purchase transaction, internal operational tooling, detailed jurisdiction rules, actual support operation, or automated decision flows because these are absent, deferred or explicitly out of MVP scope.
