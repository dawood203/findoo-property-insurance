# Define Phase — Success-Measure Proposal

**Status:** Candidate measures reproduced from the BRD. Baselines, targets, instrumentation and approval are not provided.

| Intended outcome | Candidate measure | Evidence status | Related requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easier claims | Claim-start completion rate; time to submit a complete claim; claims with visible next step. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-013–BRD-018 |
| Transparent progress | Claim-status comprehension; update-delivery rate; contacts per active claim. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-017–BRD-020 |
| Simpler application | Quote/application completion rate; median completion time; field-level abandonment. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-002–BRD-006 |
| Affordability support | Eligible users shown alternatives/discounts; conversion by approved option. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-007–BRD-010 |
| Better support | First-response time; resolution rate; post-contact satisfaction; successful human handoff. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-021–BRD-024 |
| Sustainable operations | Manual-review/rework rate; monitored accuracy/fairness of automation. | BRD candidate measure; no baseline/target | BRD-006, BRD-030 |
