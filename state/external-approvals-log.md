# External Approvals Log

DPO and EO approvals must be recorded here as external decisions. This log does not confer approval by itself.

| External approval ID | Role | Scope / artifact version | Status | Decision date | External approver | Evidence reference | Conditions / expiry | Recorded by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXT-XXX | DPO / EO / Other | Artifact path and version | Not requested / Pending / Approved / Approved with conditions / Rejected | YYYY-MM-DD or `Unknown` | Name or role | URL, receipt, export, user confirmation, or `Not evidenced` | Conditions or `None` | Name or role |

## Box evidence rule

| Item | Upload status | Evidence reference | Recorded date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Artifact path/name | Uploaded / Not evidenced / Not applicable | Verified URL, receipt/export, user confirmation, or `Not evidenced` | YYYY-MM-DD | Do not use `Uploaded` without evidence. |
