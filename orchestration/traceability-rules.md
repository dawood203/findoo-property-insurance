# Traceability Rules

1. Retain the assigned BRD IDs (`BRD-001`–`BRD-031`) from the research matrix. Do not renumber them.
2. Every requirement-derived artifact must cite one or more BRD IDs, or state `No applicable BRD requirement` with rationale.
3. Derived decisions must cite their source artifact and identify whether they are documented fact, approved decision, proposal, assumption, or open question.
4. A requirement cannot be marked satisfied solely by a design file; cite validation evidence or explicitly state that validation is pending.
5. Preserve original BRD references alongside trace IDs in the matrix.
6. Record changed, deferred, rejected or superseded requirements in the decision log with an approver/evidence reference.
7. Do not create trace links to unavailable artifacts or claim an external upload without evidence.
