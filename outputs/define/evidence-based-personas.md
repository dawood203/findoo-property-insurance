# Evidence-Based Personas

**Critical label:** These are **hypothetical, evidence-bounded proto-personas**, not validated personas. The BRD provides provisional segments only; it supplies no demographic, behavioural, attitudinal, accessibility, language, channel or market research data.

| Hypothetical proto-persona | BRD-grounded context and objective | Documented needs | Unknown / validation needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential policy shopper | Homeowner or renter seeking understandable protection at a manageable cost. | Plain coverage explanation, save/resume, understandable data requests, verified options and qualified cost information. | Segment mix, decision context, price sensitivity, property type, channels, language and accessibility needs. |
| Active claimant | Policyholder reporting property damage during a potentially stressful event. | Emergency distinction/guidance, incident/evidence capture, clear stage/owner/next action, updates and human support. | Incident types, urgency patterns, evidence capabilities, support preference, comprehension barriers and jurisdiction. |
| Existing policyholder | Customer viewing policy details, updating property information, renewing or seeking help. | Authenticated policy access, documents, dates, deductible, contact/support and approved servicing actions. | Frequency, permitted actions, renewal needs, channel/device preference and service expectations. |
| Support and claims operations user | Authorized internal user needing concise case history and reliable handoff. | Authorized context-aware summary/handoff. | Workflow, tooling, permissions, staffing, service metrics and MVP inclusion. |
