# User Needs

**Source:** BRD §§3–8  
**Evidence status:** Provisional BRD-defined needs, not validated personas or interview findings.

| Need | Relevant users | Supporting BRD requirement IDs |
|---|---|---|
| Understand protection, key terms, exclusions and deductible before purchase. | Residential policy shopper | BRD-001, BRD-008, BRD-009 |
| Complete an application with minimal unexplained effort and recover from interruptions/errors. | Residential policy shopper | BRD-002–BRD-006, BRD-026 |
| Receive only verified eligible options with qualified cost/disclosure information. | Residential policy shopper | BRD-007–BRD-012 |
| Access current policy information and permitted self-service actions securely. | Existing policyholder | BRD-011, BRD-027 |
| Start a claim safely, provide available evidence and understand what remains needed. | Active claimant | BRD-013–BRD-016 |
| Know the claim reference, stage, next action, owner and update window when known. | Active claimant | BRD-017–BRD-020 |
| Reach appropriate support or escalation without restarting or repeating unnecessary information. | All customer groups | BRD-021–BRD-023 |
| Have accessible access, clear recovery and protected data/media. | All customer groups | BRD-005, BRD-025–BRD-029 |

**Validation needed:** Which needs are highest frequency/severity, their success criteria, accessibility accommodations, channel/language needs, and differences by property segment and jurisdiction.
