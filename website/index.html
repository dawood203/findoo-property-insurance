What is Findoo?

Insurance can be complex.
Exploring it shouldn’t be.

Findoo is a proposed digital property insurance experience built around clarity, thoughtful guidance, and everyday needs.

From understanding coverage to reviewing a policy and following a claim, the concept brings information and next steps into one considered journey.

Created for an AI for Designers course, Findoo demonstrates a possible experience. It does not issue policies, provide verified quotes, or offer insurance for sale.

Understand before you choose

Know Your Coverage

General learning topics for the Findoo concept. These examples do not confirm coverage or represent an available Findoo policy.

01

Fire

A policy may address damage from specified fire events. The cause, insured property and exclusions determine whether a loss qualifies.

What to check

Read the fire-related wording, insured values, deductible and claim evidence requirements.

02

Burglary & Theft

Some contents policies include theft-related protection. Item limits, security requirements and definitions of burglary or theft can differ.

What to check

Check which belongings are insured, any declaration requirements and the insurer’s reporting process.

03

Electrical Breakdown

Electrical or mechanical failures may need a specific benefit or separate cover. Do not assume every appliance fault is insured.

What to check

Ask whether the policy addresses the equipment and failure involved, and review maintenance-related exclusions.

04

Natural Calamities

Floods, storms or earthquakes may be addressed individually in policy wording. Protection against one event does not establish protection against all disasters.

What to check

Review named events, location conditions, limits and any optional benefits before selecting a policy.

05

Manmade Hazards

Damage caused by human actions may be treated differently across policies. Some events may require additional cover or be excluded.

What to check

Ask which events are named, what restrictions apply and whether additional approval or cover is required.

06

Accidental Damage

Unexpected damage is not automatically insured. A policy must identify the relevant property, event and conditions.

What to check

Check definitions, eligible items, deductible and exclusions, including how gradual deterioration is treated.

A topic is not a promise of cover.

For a real policy, use the insurer’s full wording and schedule to verify inclusions, exclusions, limits and conditions. Findoo does not issue policies or determine claims.

Is Findoo a real insurance provider?

No. Findoo is a conceptual property insurance application created for an AI for Designers course. No insurance is available for purchase on this website.

Can I get a real quote here?

The quote preview explains the proposed journey only. It does not calculate premiums, check eligibility, or create an insurance quote.

What do the coverage categories mean?

They illustrate ways to organise building and contents information. They do not establish specific benefits, limits, exclusions, or policy availability. A real policy would require approved insurer wording.

How would the claims journey work?

The proposed journey includes reporting an incident, providing available evidence, following updates, and finding help. Actual claims rules, decisions, payments, and timelines would depend on the insurer and policy terms.

Does this website collect my information?

The contact form validates entries in your browser only. It does not transmit or save the information. Please use fictional details when trying the demo.

Is this an emergency assistance service?

No. This is a design demonstration. If you are facing an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service rather than using this website.
