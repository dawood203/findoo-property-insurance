# Usability Testing — Test Plan

**Status:** Planned scenarios only. No participant sessions, consent records, developer review, existing application screens or rendered prototypes were supplied.  
**Evidence boundary:** The scenarios evaluate proposed specifications when testable artifacts and authorization exist; they are not completed test results.

## Research questions

1. Can a target user locate and understand coverage information, material choice differences and a qualified route to apply?
2. Can a policyholder start, save and progress a claim while distinguishing emergency services from insurance support?
3. Can a customer understand claim status, next action, owner and available support/escalation without relying on unsupported guarantees?
4. Can users recover from validation, interruption, unavailable integration and unavailable real-time support states?
5. Do rendered screens meet the confirmed accessibility and responsive scope?

## Preconditions before actual sessions

- Confirm research authorization, participant consent, recruitment criteria, data handling, recording approach and session moderator.
- Provide testable rendered prototype or implemented screens, approved content, supported device/browser scope and accessibility standard.
- Confirm market/jurisdiction-specific emergency, claims, support, verification and escalation content.
- Do not collect policy, claim, payment, identity or other sensitive personal data in session artifacts unless explicitly authorized and protected.
