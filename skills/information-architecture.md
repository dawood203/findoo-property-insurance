# Skill: Information Architecture

**Objective:** Organize approved information and tasks so customers can find, understand and complete them.

**Required inputs:** Approved definition, requirement matrix, approved content/rules.

**Execution steps:**

1. Inventory required content, tasks, actors and states.
2. Model navigation, labels, hierarchy and service handoffs.
3. Map alternate, error and integration-unavailable states.
4. Trace each model element to requirements or mark it as a proposal.

**Output format:** Sitemap/navigation model, content inventory, state inventory and trace map.

**Quality checklist:** Required claim/support routes are discoverable; no unapproved content claims; all flows have exit/recovery paths.

**Restrictions:** Do not design visual UI, fabricate content approval or hide unresolved business rules.
