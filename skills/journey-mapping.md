# Skill: Journey Mapping

**Objective:** Represent documented customer tasks, touchpoints, ownership and friction across a journey.

**Required inputs:** Approved requirements, user-group evidence, service/support facts.

**Execution steps:**

1. Define actor, trigger, goal and completion condition.
2. Map steps, information needs, system/support handoffs and known failure points.
3. Identify requirements and open questions at each step.
4. Flag unsupported timing, owner or service claims as unknown.

**Output format:** Journey table/diagram with trace IDs, dependencies, risks and open questions.

**Quality checklist:** Journey reflects evidence; complete/incomplete claim states are distinguished where specified; human support is retained where required.

**Restrictions:** Do not claim emotional research, SLAs, or operational ownership without source evidence.
